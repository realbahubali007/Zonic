Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This policy explains what information may be collected when you use Zonic (), which is operated by .

Information we collect

Zonic does not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or payment details to play games. We do not sell personal information.

Like most websites, the company that hosts Zonic may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, for security and operation of the service.

Information stored on your device

Zonic stores a small value in your browser to remember that you have seen the cookie notice. It is kept on your device and is not used to identify you.

Third-party games and advertising

Games on Zonic are loaded from third-party game providers and run inside their own frames. These providers, and the advertising partners they work with, may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, measure performance, prevent fraud and save game progress. They may collect information such as your IP address, device identifiers and how you interact with the game. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read.

Cookies

See our Cookie Policy for details and for how to control cookies in your browser.

Children

Zonic is a general-audience website. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Because we collect very little directly, most requests relate to our partners. You can usually manage ad personalisation through your device settings or the privacy controls offered by the partners.

Links to other sites

Zonic may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
