Cookie Policy

Last updated:

Cookies and similar technologies are small pieces of data stored on your device. This page explains how they relate to Zonic.

What Zonic stores

Zonic itself stores only a small value in your browser's local storage to remember that you have dismissed the cookie notice.

Cookies from partner games and ads

Games are loaded from third-party providers. They and their advertising partners may set cookies or use similar technologies to deliver and measure ads, remember game progress, and prevent fraud. We do not control these cookies.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may stop certain games or ads from working properly. Many ad partners also offer their own opt-out tools for personalised ads.
