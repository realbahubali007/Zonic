Zonic respects the rights of creators. Games shown on Zonic are provided by game publishing partners through their official embed links, and remain hosted by those partners.

Reporting an infringement

If you believe a game or other content on Zonic infringes your copyright, email with:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • A description of the work you say is infringed.
  • The address (URL) of the page on Zonic where the content appears.
  • A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law.
  • A statement that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
  • Your signature (typed is fine).

What happens next

We will review the notice and, where appropriate, remove or disable access to the content and let the partner who supplies the game know. Because games are hosted by our partners, you may also need to contact the partner directly.

Please be accurate

Knowingly sending a false notice can have legal consequences.
