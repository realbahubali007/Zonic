About Zonic

Free games for everyone

Zonic is a free games portal where you can play thousands of browser games instantly. There is nothing to download and no account to create. Open a game and start playing on your phone, tablet or computer.

Where the games come from

The games on Zonic are provided by game publishing partners and are played through their official embed links. Each game, its name and its artwork belong to its developer and publisher. Zonic does not claim ownership of any partner game.

Who runs Zonic

Zonic is created and operated by . If you have a question, feedback, or a game suggestion, visit the Contact page.

Our goal

To keep playing simple: a fast, clean site, easy ways to find games you like, and no sign-up walls.
